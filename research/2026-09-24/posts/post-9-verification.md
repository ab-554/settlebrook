## Dropped or left unverified

- California PD dollar figures (weekly rate limits, weeks per percent): not included. The DWC benefits chart shows PD min $160.00 and max $290.00 for 1/1/26 injuries, but Justia's text of Lab. Code 4453(b) shows $240 to $435 for 2014+ injuries. The two conflict, and no DWC weeks-per-percent table was found, so no PD dollar figure is in the post.
- Lab. Code 4653 on Justia was not used: the extraction did not show the two-thirds rule. TD two-thirds is sourced to the DWC guidebook and chart instead.
- DWC does not say on any fetched page that I&A officers review settlements. The post only says they answer questions and help with claim problems.
- DEU Form 110 says a C&R closes everything "except vocational rehabilitation." That form is dated 06-05, so the post leaves that exception out.
- The 5-year reopening window under stips has exceptions and case-law nuance (for example good cause under 5803). The post states the general DWC rule only.
- Lab. Code text should be re-checked on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov before publish.
