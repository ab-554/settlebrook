## Claims checked and CONFIRMED accurate (no discrepancy — listed for completeness, not required output but useful QA trail)

- Ohio 25/50/25 minimum liability and non-economic cap ($250k or 3x economic, max $350k) — confirmed via codes.ohio.gov and bmv.ohio.gov
- Nevada 25/50/20 minimum liability — confirmed via doi.nv.gov
- Washington 25/50/10 minimum liability — confirmed via RCW 46.29.090 (page only states the BI figures, doesn't contradict)
- All 2-year SOL claims (CA, TX, FL, IL, PA, GA, OH, AZ, NV) and 3-year SOL claims (NY, WA, MI, NC) — confirmed against each state's statute
- Comparative/contributory fault rules and bar percentages for CA, TX, FL, IL, PA, GA, OH, AZ, WA, CO, NV — confirmed against statute text
- Florida PIP $10,000 minimum, NY PIP $50,000 and serious-injury threshold, NY 90-day notice of claim — confirmed against statute
- Michigan mini-tort $3,000 (post-7/1/2020) — confirmed via MCL 500.3135
- Colorado non-economic damages cap raised to $1.5M for claims filed on/after 1/1/2025 (HB24-1472) — confirmed
- Florida HB 837 (2023): SOL cut from 4 to 2 years, comparative fault changed to 51% bar — confirmed against Fla. Stat. §§ 95.11(5)(a) and 768.81
