## Notes
- CA: exception is 240 weeks/5 yrs for HIV, hep B/C, amputation, severe burns, high-velocity/chemical eye injury, pulmonary fibrosis, chronic lung disease (Lab. Code §4656(c)(3)).
- TX: TIBs alone stop at MMI (§408.101); the 401-week figure is the outer cap on TIBs+IIBs+SIBs combined, from date of injury (occupational disease: from date benefits began to accrue).
- IL: §8(b) has no week-based cap for TTD itself (unlike PPD, which uses scheduled-week multipliers).
- PA: Act 111 (2018) reinstated IREs; if whole-body impairment ≥35% under the 6th edition AMA Guides, total disability status continues past 104 weeks instead of converting to partial.
- GA: catastrophic designation under O.C.G.A. §34-9-200.1 removes the 400-week limit.
- OH: BWC/self-insured employer "may" schedule an exam at 200 weeks; TTD continues if the disability is not found permanent.
- NC: applies to injuries on/after June 24, 2011 (S.B. 709); extended compensation requires proving total loss of wage-earning capacity, reviewable every 2 years.
- VA: §65.2-500 (permanent total) is unlimited/lifetime; the 500-week cap in §65.2-518 applies to total compensation generally (TTD + PPD combined), not to TTD in isolation.
- MN: 130-week limit counts initial + recommenced TTD combined, paused only during a Board-approved retraining plan (§176.102 subd. 11).
